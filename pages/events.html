    Get tips on part scholarships & information on visa regulations

    Studying abroad does not always mean paying full tuition. At the event, you will discover a range of tuition discount opportunities offered by participating universities in the UK and Canada. In addition, attendees will receive valuable guidance on current student visa regulations and immigration requirements.

    Meet schools with 100% study loans in the UK and Canada.

    Financing an international education can be one of the biggest concerns for prospective students. At our Study Abroad Fair, you will have the opportunity to know more on financial partners offering up to 100% study loan options for eligible students.

    Get answers to all your questions in one place.

    Instead of spending weeks searching online for information from multiple sources, you can have all your questions answered during the event. Whether your concerns relate to admissions, tuition fees, part scholarships, accommodation, visa applications, employment opportunities, or student life, experts will be available to provide clear and reliable guidance

    Come with your credentials and other documents.

    To make the most of your experience at the fair, we encourage you to come prepared with your academic credentials and other relevant supporting documents. Bring items such as international passport, CV and credentials.

    Get admission assessment and offer on the spot.

    Receive a personalized admission assessment directly from university representatives and experienced education consultants. By presenting your academic credentials and supporting documents, you can obtain immediate feedback on your eligibility for various programmes.
